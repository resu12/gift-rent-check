Gift Rent Check privacy notice

Invitation beta · Updated 10 October 2026

Gift Rent Check is a read-only gift rental dashboard hosted on Telegram Serverless. Invited accounts use their own wallet and Marketapp API key. The original owner's private dashboard is kept separate.

Data used

The app uses Telegram's verified numeric user ID to select your account. It saves your verified TON wallet address, wallet approval challenge metadata, encrypted Marketapp key, public blockchain observations, collection and rental records, gift portfolio, sync progress and comparison results. Public provider pages can contain information about other wallets and gifts. The app does not request your wallet seed phrase, private key or a transaction.

Storage and access

Account data is stored in the Telegram backend. API keys are encrypted before storage and are not returned to the browser or included in downloads. This is application encryption, not end-to-end encryption: the app operator controls the backend and encryption key. Saved gift and price data is available only through your Telegram account's scope; the operator and hosting provider retain administrative access.

External services

Manual and automatic refreshes send your API key only to Marketapp's authenticated API, and wallet and gift addresses to TON Center. Opening the app can refresh saved gifts and comparison data when the required connections and allowances are available. Wallet connection uses TON Connect and your chosen wallet. These providers handle requests under their own policies. Public personal website analytics is disabled. The dashboard makes no automatic price changes or transactions.

Retention and your controls

Evidence is retained until you choose Delete my data, subject to the beta's storage allowance. Removing a key revokes it and stops work that needs it. Disconnecting a wallet keeps your saved gifts. Export saved gifts downloads dashboard and job data, not a full raw-evidence backup.

Delete my data blocks your account immediately and removes keys, wallet verification, gift records and progress in bounded batches. If cleanup remains pending, reload account settings until it finishes. Non-secret security counters, account tombstones and request usage remain to prevent replay and limit resets. Public-account deletion does not erase operator backups or the original owner's separate private dataset. Contact the person who invited you about backup retention or other privacy questions.

Invitation access

Only explicitly invited Telegram accounts can enroll. Using your wallet does not grant another Telegram account access to your saved data. Contact the person who invited you if your numeric Telegram ID needs updating.
