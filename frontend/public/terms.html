Gift Rent Check beta terms

Invitation beta · Updated 10 October 2026

This experimental dashboard helps invited users inspect their own TON gifts and compare observed daily rental prices. Use a wallet you control and a Marketapp API key you are authorized to use. Keep your key private and follow Marketapp, TON Center and Telegram's requirements.

Read-only operation

Wallet verification proves control of your wallet for this dashboard. It does not submit a transaction. The app does not change listing prices, rent gifts or schedule transactions. Each invited account begins with an empty portfolio and its own credentials. Connecting a wallet alone does not import gifts.

Estimates and incomplete data

Recommendations are comparisons of available observations, not guaranteed earnings or a market valuation. Samples, indexer delays, incomplete scans and unknown contracts can affect results. Contract terms do not prove that a listing is visible on Marketapp, and an expired rental does not prove that a gift has returned. Check the displayed observation times and uncertainty before acting in Marketapp.

Limits and availability

The beta can pause syncs at request, runtime, gift or storage limits. Limits apply to individual accounts and the shared service; another account can use shared capacity. API keys do not bypass these limits. Saved progress can be continued when capacity becomes available. Features and invitation access may change during the beta.

Account controls

You can remove your API key, disconnect verification, export saved dashboard data, or delete your account data from account settings. Deletion runs in bounded batches and retains non-secret security and request counters. Read the privacy notice before connecting your wallet or saving a key. Contact the person who invited you for support.
